USER MANUAL

1. DEPLOY SATELLITE: Procurement begins on startup. Select exactly one (1) probe class to enter Heliocentric orbit.

2. SUN PHOTOGRAPHY: Click the primary "CAPTURE PHOTO" button at the bottom center to command the probe to take a spectral photograph of the Sun. Captured thumbnails will appear in the top-right corner.

4. DATA TELEMETRY: Telemetry indicators will gather temperature readings and solar wind speeds automatically. Complete all objectives to finalize the mission.
